# Data Privacy Guarantees

## 1. Absolute Local Data Sovereignty

* **Your data never leaves your computer**: Invoices, trade names, taxable turnovers, and tax liability computations remain strictly on the local workstation.
* **No Telemetry**: No analytics libraries, crash-tracking beacons, or tracking cookies are included in the codebase.
* **Audit Trail Exclusivity**: All activity logs and user review notes remain inside the local SQLite database.
* **Data Erasure**: Deleting a project within the application purges all associated records and matches from the local database.
