# Quick Start Guide (5-Minute Walkthrough)

Follow these simple steps to reconcile your first pair of GST files:

### Step 1: Open the App and Click "Load Demo Data"
If you don't have files ready, click **Load Demo Data** on the Dashboard. This populates a sample GSTR-2B and Purchase Register containing exact matches, tax differences, missing records, and formatting variations.

### Step 2: Or Start a "New Reconciliation"
1. Click **New Reconciliation** on the sidebar.
2. Select your **GSTR-2B Excel File** as Source A.
3. Select your **Purchase Register Excel/CSV** as Source B.
4. Click **Next**.

### Step 3: Confirm Columns
The application will automatically detect your columns (GSTIN, Invoice Number, Date, Taxable Value, CGST, SGST, IGST). Verify the selections and click **Next**.

### Step 4: Run Reconciliation
Review the default tolerances (₹5.00 taxable, ₹2.00 tax). Click **Run Reconciliation**.

### Step 5: Review & Export
Inspect the Dashboard for your **ITC at Risk**. Switch to the **Issue Explorer** to see individual discrepancies, or click **Export to Excel** to save your final audit report!
